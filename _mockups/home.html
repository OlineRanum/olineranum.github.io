Oline A. Ranum

PhD researcher · Centre for Vision, Speech and Signal Processing, University of Surrey

My research focuses on Sign Language Processing, Recognition, and Translation, with a particular interest in spatial grammar and productive constructions.

I'm supervised by Dr. Richard Bowden and Dr. Simon Hadfield.

  • WACV 2027

    Beyond BLEU: A Case for Redefining Sign Language Translation Benchmarks

    An evaluation study across six SLT systems showing that gains in BLEU-4 are not on their own evidence of better sign understanding.

    PaperProject page
  • Annu. Rev. Ling.

    Machine Translation for Sign Languages

  • ECCV 2026

    BackTranslation2.0: A Linguistically Motivated Metric to Assess Sign Language Production

    A linguistically grounded, agentic metric for text-to-sign translation, validated against native Deaf raters on BSL.

    PaperProject page
  • Under review

    What's the Point? Spatial Grammar & Index Resolution for Sign Language Processing

    A pose-based index detector and online entity memory that substantially improve pointing-sign recovery in CSLR without retraining the backbone.
