<strong>BackTranslation2.0: A Linguistically Motivated Metric to Assess Sign Language Production</strong> was accepted at <strong>ECCV 2026</strong>.

<br><br>

<strong>Venue:</strong> ECCV 2026<br>

[🌐 Project Page](https://cogvis-cvssp.github.io/BackTranslation2/){:target="_blank"} &nbsp;·&nbsp; [📄 Paper (PDF)](https://cogvis-cvssp.github.io/BackTranslation2/assets/BackTranslation2_0___ECCV_2026_Submission.pdf){:target="_blank"}

**Abstract:** BackTranslation2.0 introduces a linguistically grounded evaluation metric for text-to-sign translation. The approach uses an agentic framework in which a deterministic pipeline orchestrates a suite of specialised tools to assess grammatical correctness, phonological accuracy, motion fluency, and generation fidelity. Using cross-referential LLM-based comparisons and validated on a British Sign Language dataset annotated by native Deaf raters, the metric demonstrates stronger correlation with human judgements than six baseline metrics.
